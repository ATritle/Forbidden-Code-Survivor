FORBIDDEN CODE: SURVIVOR - WINDOWS 0.4.3

COMPACT LOADOUT WORKBENCH

Player selection remains on the left and decks on the right. The same eight
cards fit in a 4-by-2 grid, now 210 x 200 logical pixels instead of 251 x 244
(16% narrower, 18% shorter). Artwork, buttons and rider rows have been tightened
individually, with wider outside margins showing the neon city.

Cards keep their name, magic school, damage and cooldown visible. Click a card
for its full description, evolution details and deck/start-slot editing.
School filters and the three saved deck selectors sit above the grid. The
level-up pool, named starting attacks, Save Deck and Enter Arena sit below it.
The existing music dock, strong silent hover shading and click sounds remain.
Settings, How To Play, gameplay HUD and combat balance retain their sizes.

VALIDATION

Playback exercises real rendered button hit testing, all three saved decks,
card unlock/replacement, two starters, rider choice, filters and paging, modal
blocking and Enter Arena at 1280x720, 1600x900 and 2560x1440. Separate restarts
verify saved selection. Pixel checks measure hover shading in actual captures.
Guide/quit buttons, music controls/repeat/persistence, click audio, a packaged
run and an install/launch/uninstall roundtrip are verified in the release
validation folder. These are local automated checks, not broad hardware QA.

RUN AND REVERT

Play-Forbidden-Routines.cmd launches Windows 0.4.3.
Play-Windows-0.4.2.cmd launches the preceding, preserved menu layout.
Artifacts/Windows-0.4.3/ForbiddenCodeSurvivor.exe is the portable package.
Artifacts/Installer-0.4.3/ForbiddenCode-Survivor-0.4.3-Setup.exe is the installer.
Earlier packages/installers and the original baseline archive remain intact;
the release manifest records file hashes and preservation verification.
No browser deployment, new generated artwork or Meshy credits are involved.
