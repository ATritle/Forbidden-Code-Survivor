NO PAIN - NO GAIN: NEON EXORCIST CHALLENGE STATION
Concept proposal v1. Balance values are starting points for playtesting.
These are design options, not changes to the current playable build.

PLAYER FLOW
Approach the lit street shrine: [E] NO PAIN - NO GAIN.
Open a glass-and-neon contract panel with the challenge's name, reward,
explicit penalty, duration, and an animated preview of its danger.
Buttons: ACCEPT CHALLENGE / DECLINE. Opening the panel pauses gameplay;
closing it restores the previous pause/input state. No damage can occur
behind the decision panel. Declining has no cost.
Only one contract can be active. The station shows ACTIVE / COMPLETE / FAILED
and cannot be used repeatedly to stack bonuses. A small HUD contract tracker
shows the remaining duration and the condition that triggers the penalty.

1. OVERLOAD ORB (the player's proposed mechanic)
Reward: triple outgoing spell damage for up to 45 seconds.
Risk: a visibly tethered neon orb circles the player. An active enemy touching
the orb ruptures it, instantly dealing 50% of the player's MAXIMUM health to
the player and immediately removing the damage bonus. The rupture can kill
the player if their remaining health is 50% or less.
Readability: bright pink core, cyan vulnerability ring, orbit-path preview.
Rules: one rupture only, 1-second arming grace, no damage to the player from
the orb simply touching walls. The orb must remain within camera framing.
The contract penalty bypasses ordinary contact invulnerability. Talisman
instant kills do not prevent an orb contact already confirmed that frame.
Starting orbit radius: 180 cm; trial radius and enemy contacts in the range.

2. UNSTABLE ECHO
Reward: every third successful spell hit creates an additional energy blast
dealing twice that hit's damage to enemies within 180 cm.
Risk: the blast charges visibly for 1 second, then also deals 20% max HP to
the player if they are inside its marked circle. Active for 40 seconds.
Readability: cyan hit flash followed by an amber-to-magenta expanding danger
ring. It rewards firing while moving rather than standing in a dense pack.
Rules: at most one echo can be created every 0.5 seconds; echo damage cannot
create more echoes. Show actual collision radius before detonation.

3. HOSTILE BROADCAST
Reward: double essence earned from enemies defeated during a 60-second trial.
Risk: the player's location is broadcast to corporate hunters. Two additional
elite hunters arrive every 15 seconds, with clear edge-of-screen warnings.
Readability: orange holographic bounty marker above the hoverboard rider,
cyan essence trails, visible countdown to each reinforcement.
Rules: respect the existing active-enemy cap and pool; reserve/reassign spawn
slots for the hunters. Do not silently expand the actor pool or stop combat.

4. DEADMAN BATTERY
Reward: double outgoing spell damage for 30 seconds.
Risk: the contract continuously drains 2% max HP per second. Each confirmed
enemy kill restores 4% max HP. Health drain can kill the player.
Readability: yellow energy meter visibly drains; each kill sends a green-cyan
charge back to the player. This favors a strong crowd-clearing loadout and
becomes dangerous when fighting isolated high-health enemies.
Rules: healing triggers once per real enemy death, never per hit, illusion,
summon, respawned training dummy, or secondary damage event.

5. GLASS PROTOCOL
Reward: projectiles gain two extra enemy penetrations and all spell damage
increases by 75% for 45 seconds.
Risk: all damage the player receives is doubled for the same duration.
Readability: a thin fractured holographic shell surrounds the player; hits
send harmless visual cracks across it. Clear fixed reward and fixed penalty
make this a straightforward first challenge for players to understand.
Rules: damage multipliers are applied once through the central damage path.
The two extra penetrations only affect spells that support piercing.

6. JACKPOT COURIER
Reward: collect an unstable data cache and return to this station within
35 seconds to earn one bonus three-card upgrade selection.
Risk: carrying the cache broadcasts the player and increases pursuing enemy
movement speed by 25%. Failing to deliver triggers 35% max-HP backlash.
Readability: amber route markers, a hovering pink cache behind the player,
cyan station beacon, clear timer and delivery radius.
Rules: select a reachable cache position using navigation distance, not a
random point through a wall. The movement modifier is removed on every
success, failure, death, or map-exit path. The reward obeys existing deck
eligibility; it does not invent permanent card unlocks or alter saved loadouts.

7. LOCKDOWN DUEL
Reward: defeat a marked elite inside a 900 cm arena ring within 40 seconds
to earn a guaranteed bonus upgrade draft and an essence cache.
Risk: stepping outside the clearly marked ring for more than 2 seconds fails
the contract and causes 35% max-HP backlash. Normal enemies still approach.
Readability: cyan ring turns amber while the escape grace timer runs; the
elite has a separate magenta contract marker and health bar.
Rules: choose a navigable, unobstructed ring site. No invisible blocking wall;
the player can deliberately escape and accept the cost. Bosses are excluded
from the initial version. Apply reward or failure exactly once.

RECOMMENDED FIRST PLAYTEST
Overload Orb: vulnerable orbit and very high damage reward.
Unstable Echo: movement and spatial awareness.
Hostile Broadcast: escalating horde pressure and faster progression.
Start with one offered contract per station; randomized three-contract offers
can follow once each individual contract is readable and balanced.

IMPLEMENTATION OUTLINE FOR A LATER APPROVED GAMEPLAY PASS
Use an AChallengeStation interactable, data assets for contract definitions,
and a player-owned UChallengeComponent. States: Available, Offered, Active,
Succeeded, Failed. Apply temporary modifiers through removable handles rather
than permanently changing saved card data. Centralize damage, kill and timer
hooks; clear modifiers and spawned contract actors on every terminal state.
Store a run identifier and activation identifier so repeated overlap/click
callbacks cannot multiply effects, award duplicate drafts or duplicate damage.
Use game time for trial countdowns so settings/draft pauses stop the challenge.
Training targets cannot award contract kills or permanent rewards.
Damage and danger telegraphs stay readable even at zero impact intensity.

SIGN ART / MESHY HANDOFF
Use the isolated three-quarter prop concept image as the model reference.
Generate the solid housing, supports, weighted base, protective ring and cables.
Rebuild the exact NO PAIN - NO GAIN lettering as separate emissive geometry
or a clean masked emissive sign texture in UE; do not depend on generated
geometry preserving letter shapes perfectly. Separate material slots:
dark housing, pink tubes, amber tubes, cyan trim, energy core, touchpad.
Animate emissive intensity in material instances: slow soft breathing, a
short confirmation sweep on accept, and an amber failure flicker. Use a few
controlled local lights for nearby illumination; emissive alone is not a
guaranteed stable light source for every Lumen scene and camera distance.
Keep collision simple on the base/housing. Lettering, cables and the neon
core are visual-only. Provide a clear walk-up area and interaction trigger.
