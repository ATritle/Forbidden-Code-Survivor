# Cybernetic enemy roster

Ten original cyborg concepts for Neon Exorcist. Every image was generated individually using the built-in image-generation tool. Full prompts and Meshy provenance are saved beside the concepts in `ContentSource/Enemies/CyberRoster`. Meshy conversion: approved maximum 200 credits, T2 textured FBX plus rigs and included walk/run clips; no paid retries.

Ordinary enemies join a weighted mix only after their unlock time. The Broadcast Sovereign is admitted once per run at or after 360 seconds, through the next successful pool activation. It currently uses ground pursuit and contact damage; bespoke boss attack phases are a later content pass. Larger enemies have larger collision radii, floor clearance, visual scale, and health. Their active pool remains capped at 180.

All ten approved models and rigs were generated successfully for **200 Meshy credits total**. Basic through elite sources contain 5,826–6,387 triangles; the boss contains 11,660. Each has a 24-bone rig, walk/run clips, three generated skeletal LODs, and PBR maps with selective neon emission. The gameplay importer explicitly saves the generated skeleton as a separate asset package before saving animation references.
