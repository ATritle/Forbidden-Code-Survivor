RUN STRUCTURE
Two optional elevated relay hacks permit the Sovereign broadcast at 03:00. Otherwise it arrives at 06:00.
Defeat the Sovereign to win. Victory and defeat show the build, confirmed damage contribution, contracts and final score.
Recovered code buys horizontal program unlocks. Wins unlock a spectral board and Blackout district variant.
Three researched captures unlock a spectral board without the Blackout difficulty modifier.
Blackout adds elite patrols after 03:00, dims street lights by 25% and awards 20% extra essence. Character readability fills remain enabled.

MAP AND MOVEMENT
Second approaches connect the market, promenade and rooftop shrine. Lower camera-facing buildings and larger visual cutaways reveal combat approaches.
Simple collision proxies remain separate from decorative Meshy geometry. The largest enemy is included in terrain-route validation.
Explore caches, a salvage-funded clinic, the shrine ward defense and telegraphed subway power surges.
New textured shopfront and service modules add recessed doors, shutters, equipment and city detail. Animated digital pedestrians replace the primitive spirits.
Puddles favor gutters and smaller patches, with rougher aggregate between reflections.
Boost provides a short swept hoverboard acceleration burst on a three-second cooldown.
Large landings stagger nearby enemies, with reduced boss stagger and an eight-second anti-farming cooldown.
The rider uses a blended Animation Blueprint for riding, turning, casting, falling, landing, hits and defeat.

DECK AND DRAFTS
Choose an eight-program draft pool and two starters. A run holds six active programs.
Taking a seventh program requires an explicit replacement. Replacement removes the old weapon and its talisman charges where relevant.
Three saved presets, two rerolls and one banish support curated builds.
Programs have three finite ranks followed by one of two mutually exclusive structural evolutions.
Fully evolved programs leave the offer pool; consumable talismans can still receive effective refills below the eight-charge cap.
Card previews show current and next damage/cooldown and the structural change. Rarity is independent of magic school.
Talisman charges execute basic/medium/elite enemies at costs 1/2/3. Four charges deal 8% boss max HP and a brief stagger. They cannot execute bosses or trigger execute-derived Echo damage.

ENEMIES AND CONTRACTS
The ten cyborgs have matching Meshy combat clips. Roles include swarm, intercept, directional armor, disruption zones, directional ally shielding, charges, interruptible support, crossing dashes, area denial and a three-phase boss.
The Sovereign begins with destructible shielding nodes, sweeps broadcast lanes in phase two, then breaches digital spirits in phase three. District markers react to the broadcast.
Telegraphs and recovery windows remain visible regardless of cosmetic particle density. Defensive mechanics reduce damage rather than invalidating entire builds.
All seven No Pain - No Gain contracts remain in the random offer bag.
Echo is bounded and non-recursive. Battery heals 1% max HP per kill, at most three times per second. Broadcast difficulty scales with time and loadout. Glass explicitly warns about doubled incoming damage.
Courier objectives use reachable routes and directional guidance. Duel placement validates a flat, connected fighting space before consuming a sign.
Success offers a run-long corrupt perk with an explicit downside, or the option to bank salvage without corruption.
Capture enemy code for twenty seconds of assault or slowing support, or research it for permanent unlock progress.

CONTROLS AND READABILITY
WASD moves; Shift boosts; wheel zooms; T cycles nearest/strongest/marked targeting; middle click marks; E interacts; Tab inspects; Escape opens settings; L opens the Windows leaderboard.
Movement, boost, marking, interaction and inspect keys can be rebound. Target preference and zoom persist.
Controller left stick moves, right stick zooms, A boosts/selects, X interacts, Start opens settings, Back opens the leaderboard. D-pad/Enter/A navigate decision surfaces.
Flash, bloom, camera shake, damage-number density and impact-particle density are separate settings. Reduced Flashes limits light flashes and bloom while preserving danger information.
Priority warnings use a reserved audio voice and duck routine attack/impact sounds. The low-health sound fades after five seconds; the edge warning remains until recovery.
The compact six-slot HUD shows cooldowns, with detailed inspection on demand. Card art stays square and the updated draft surface uses glass/neon styling.

PERFORMANCE AND SCORES
Gameplay pools and cosmetic admission are measured separately. Cosmetic saturation never removes confirmed damage feedback or changes damage calculations.
Crowd pose evaluation is full rate nearby, 30Hz at medium distance and 15Hz farther away; bosses remain full rate. Movement, collision and AI are independent of pose frequency.
The public Windows leaderboard is separate from the browser board. Each local player identity retains its three highest runs; the board displays ten scores.
Posting requires an explicit username submission after a registered online run. Offline runs remain in local history.
