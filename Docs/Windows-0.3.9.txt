Forbidden Code: Survivor - Windows 0.3.9 / Repeat selected menu track

The menu music player now restarts the selected song at natural completion,
including resetting its displayed playhead and music spectrum timing. It keeps
repeating until the user selects a different song through previous, next or
TRACKS. Pause/resume, seeking, volume and mute retain their behavior.

The gameplay playlist still advances normally and continues through card drafts.
Music assets are unchanged. Menu completion repeats the same track, with a
pause/seek-aware real-time fallback because paused worlds can delay the engine
completion delegate. The timer and visualizer restart with the sound.

Packaged validation lets every one of the four menu tracks finish twice and
checks playback, selected index and restarted playhead. It also pauses near
track end, tests all manual controls, verifies saved volume/mute after restart,
records audible mixer output and checks gameplay rotation/draft continuity.

The current launcher opens 0.3.9. Play-Windows-0.3.8.cmd retains the previous
build. Versioned packages/installers and the original baseline remain separate.
